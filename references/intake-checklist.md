# Intake checklist

Inspect supplied images, product pages, text files, and earlier messages before asking questions.

## Blocking information

| Input | Acceptable evidence | Why it is required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product identity | At least one product image/video or exact visual description | Prevents changed product, packaging, color, and logo. |
| Verified selling points | Product page, specification, or user-confirmed facts | Prevents unsupported claims. |
| Restrictions | Brand guidance, marketplace restrictions, or user confirmation | Prevents unsafe/deceptive visuals and claims. |
| Audience and market | Buyer persona and destination market/language | Determines scenario, captions, and CTA. |

## Defaults requiring disclosure

- 30 seconds, vertical 9:16.
- 3 × 10-second plan; use 6 × 5 seconds for fragile product-action shots.
- Everyday home scenario and product-link CTA.
- No spoken claims beyond supplied facts.

Ask only for missing blocking information in one concise batch. Do not ask for an optional choice when a disclosed default can safely proceed.
